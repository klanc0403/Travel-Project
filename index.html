Sightseeing

Lyon offers a rich blend of history, architecture, and modern culture, making it one of France’s most dynamic cities to explore. From the striking design of the Musée des Confluences to the sweeping views at Notre-Dame de Fourvière and the ancient Roman heritage of Lugdunum, there’s something to discover at every turn. Whether you’re interested in museums, historic landmarks, or simply wandering through charming neighborhoods, Lyon invites you to experience its layers of past and present.

Bouchons lyonnais

No visit to Lyon is complete without experiencing its famous bouchon restaurants, where tradition and hospitality come together over hearty, authentic cuisine. These intimate, family-style establishments serve classic Lyonnais dishes that reflect the city’s deep culinary roots. From beloved spots like Café des Fédérations to hidden gems like Le Musée, bouchons offer a warm, lively atmosphere and a true taste of local culture that goes far beyond a typical dining experience.

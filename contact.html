Lyon Office de Tourisme

The Lyon Office of Tourism is the city’s central hub for visitors looking to make the most of their stay. Located in the heart of Lyon at Place Bellecour, it offers helpful resources including maps, guided tour bookings, event information, and expert local recommendations. Whether you’re planning your itinerary or looking for last-minute ideas, the tourism office provides reliable, up-to-date guidance to help you experience Lyon with confidence.
